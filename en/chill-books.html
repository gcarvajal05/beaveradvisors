01 · Beaver Enterprise

Beaver Enterprise

Accounting solutions for companies with more complex operations.

As a company grows, its financial processes become more demanding. Managing inventory, imports, costs, multiple operations, and corporate structures calls for accounting that goes beyond recording transactions.

Beaver Enterprise is our specialized solution for organizations that need more robust accounting management, structured financial information, and greater visibility into their operations.

We combine accounting expertise, knowledge of business processes, and technology tools to deliver reliable information that supports how the business is run.

Who is this service for?

For commercial companies, distributors, importers, manufacturers, and organizations whose operations require specialized accounting treatment.

Especially those with one or more of the following characteristics:

  • Inventory management and cost of sales.
  • Imports and foreign currency transactions.
  • Different product lines, services, or business units.
  • Multiple bank accounts and financing sources.
  • More complex cost and expense structures.
  • Need for detailed financial information for management.

What's included?

Specialized end-to-end accountingRecording, classification, and review of accounting transactions according to the nature of the business and the applicable financial reporting framework.
Reconciliations and account analysisPeriodic review of bank accounts, ledger balances, and relevant financial statement items.
Inventory accountingAccounting recording and reconciliation of inventory movements, costs, and adjustments, in line with the company's systems and controls.
Accounting for importsRecording of international purchases, import-related costs, obligations, and foreign currency transactions.
Cost accountingAccounting classification and analysis of costs related to products, services, or operating activities.
Asset and liability controlAccounting follow-up of fixed assets, depreciation, financing, and obligations.
Financial statementsPreparation of the income statement, balance sheet, statement of changes in equity, cash flows, and explanatory notes, under the applicable financial reporting framework.
Tax compliancePreparation and filing of the tax obligations included in the service, considering the specifics of the operation: VAT, income tax, and the Beneficial Ownership Transparency Registry.
Segment financial reportingInformation by cost center, business line, or operating unit, when the record structure allows it.
Financial follow-up meetingsReview of results, relevant variances, and accounting matters that require management's attention.

What you get

An accounting structure ready to meet the demands of your operation, improve the reliability of your information, and make financial oversight of the business easier.

The greater the complexity, the greater the need for control.

02 · Micro and small businesses

Accounting for micro and small businesses

Your business is growing. Your accounting should evolve too.

Running a business takes much more than knowing how much you sold during the month. You need to understand your expenses, know your results, and keep financial control of your operations.

At Beaver Advisors we support micro and small commercial and service businesses with organized, timely accounting management tailored to their operating reality.

Our goal is for you to have reliable financial information so you can run your business with more confidence.

Who is this service for?

For micro and small commercial or service businesses that need to outsource their accounting and keep proper control of their operations. Ideal for businesses with suppliers, customers, business bank accounts, operating expenses, and an administrative structure that is still developing.

See what's included 9 services
  • Comprehensive monthly accounting: recording and classification of the company's economic transactions.
  • Bank reconciliations: monthly review of bank accounts and financial movements.
  • Financial statements: monthly preparation of the income statement, balance sheet, statement of changes in equity, cash flows, and explanatory notes, with information that shows how the business is performing.
  • Tax compliance: preparation and filing of tax returns according to your tax regime (VAT, income tax, and the Beneficial Ownership Transparency Registry).
  • Accounts receivable and payable control: recording and control of outstanding balances with customers and suppliers.
  • Asset and obligation records: accounting for fixed assets, loans, and other financial obligations.
  • Business performance analysis: review of revenue, costs, expenses, and relevant variances.
  • Digital accounting tools: information management through accounting systems that make records easier to organize and consult.
  • Follow-up meetings: regular sessions to review financial results and answer relevant questions.

What you get

Organized accounting that lets you understand your company's financial position, meet your obligations, and have useful information to manage its growth.

Because a business that knows its numbers can make better decisions.

03 · Nonprofit organizations

Accounting for nonprofit organizations

Financial transparency for organizations that create impact.

Nonprofit organizations carry a special responsibility: to manage their resources transparently and show how those resources advance their mission.

At Beaver Advisors we offer specialized accounting services for foundations, associations, and other nonprofit entities, taking into account the specifics of their funding sources, activities, and reporting obligations.

We help you keep organized records, strengthen accountability, and have reliable financial information to support the management of your organization.

Who is this service for?

For foundations, associations, and nonprofit organizations that manage donations, contributions, projects, social programs, or other funding sources.

See what's included 9 services
  • Specialized monthly accounting: recording and classification of transactions according to the nature of the organization and the applicable accounting framework.
  • Donation and contribution records: accounting for resources received, considering their nature and associated conditions.
  • Accounting control by project and fund: classification of income and expenses by program, project, or funding source, according to the entity's needs.
  • Restricted resource tracking: accounting identification of funds subject to specific conditions or purposes.
  • Bank reconciliations: monthly review of bank movements and balances.
  • Financial statements: preparation of the income statement, balance sheet, statement of changes in equity, cash flows, and explanatory notes, under the applicable financial reporting framework.
  • Compliance with obligations: preparation and filing of tax returns and reports included in the service, according to applicable obligations.
  • Management reporting: financial information that makes it easier for boards of directors and project leads to oversee resources.
  • Accounting guidance: advice on recording, classifying, and presenting transactions specific to nonprofit organizations.

What you get

Clear, organized, and reliable financial information to strengthen transparency, make accountability easier, and support your organization's sustainability.

You focus on creating impact. We help you look after the resources that make it possible.

04 · Independent professionals

Accounting for independent professionals

You focus on your profession. We take care of your accounting.

Working independently means much more than delivering excellent service. It also means managing your income, controlling your expenses, and keeping your tax obligations up to date.

At Beaver Advisors we simplify your accounting so you can spend more time on what you do best, with the peace of mind of professional support.

Who is this service for?

Designed for independent professionals, consultants, freelancers, and individuals who carry out economic activities on their own account.

See what's included 7 services
  • Bookkeeping and classification: monthly organization of income, expenses, and other transactions related to your economic activity.
  • Bank reconciliation: review of the bank movements tied to your activity to keep records reliable.
  • Tax compliance: preparation and filing of the periodic and annual tax returns included in the service, according to your obligations.
  • Deductible expense control: identification and classification of expenses related to your economic activity, in line with applicable regulations.
  • Financial summary: clear information about your income, expenses, and results.
  • Digital accounting tools: use of technology solutions to make organizing and managing your information easier.
  • Professional support: answers to accounting and tax questions related to your activity.

What you get

Peace of mind, organization, and greater clarity about your professional finances, without having to spend your own time managing the accounting.
